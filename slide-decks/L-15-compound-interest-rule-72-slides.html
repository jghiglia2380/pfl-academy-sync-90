Key Terms

4 / 20
Principal
Definition

The initial amount of money you deposit or invest — your starting point

Example

You deposit $1,000 into a savings account. That $1,000 is your principal.

Simple Interest
Definition

Interest calculated only on the original principal amount, never changing

Example

$1,000 at 5% simple interest = $50/year, every year (always calculated on $1,000)

Key Terms

5 / 20
Compound Interest
Definition

Interest earned on both the principal AND previously earned interest — "interest on interest"

Example

$1,000 at 5%: Year 1 = $1,050, Year 2 = $1,102.50 (5% of $1,050, not $1,000)

Rule of 72
Definition

A formula to estimate how many years it takes for money to double: 72 ÷ interest rate (%)

Example

At 6% interest: 72 ÷ 6 = 12 years to double your money

Simple vs. Compound Interest

6 / 20

The $1,822 Difference

Let's see how $1,000 grows over 30 years at 5% interest using each method:

  • Simple Interest: $1,000 + ($1,000 × 5% × 30) = $2,500
  • Compound Interest: $1,000 × (1.05)^30 = $4,322
  • The difference: $1,822 extra from compounding!
  • Compound interest grows exponentially, not linearly
  • The longer the time, the bigger the gap

Visualizing the Difference

7 / 20

📊 Simple Interest

Growth follows a straight line — the same dollar amount each year.

  • Year 1: $1,000 + $50 = $1,050
  • Year 10: $1,000 + $500 = $1,500
  • Year 20: $1,000 + $1,000 = $2,000
  • Year 30: $1,000 + $1,500 = $2,500

📈 Compound Interest

Growth curves upward — accelerating faster each year.

  • Year 1: $1,000 × 1.05 = $1,050
  • Year 10: $1,000 × 1.05^10 = $1,629
  • Year 20: $1,000 × 1.05^20 = $2,653
  • Year 30: $1,000 × 1.05^30 = $4,322

The Rule of 72

8 / 20

A Shortcut to Estimate Doubling Time

The Formula
Years to Double = 72 ÷ Interest Rate (%)

This simple formula helps you quickly estimate how long it takes for any investment to double based on its interest rate. No calculator needed!

72 ÷ 3 = 24
Years to double at 3% interest
72 ÷ 6 = 12
Years to double at 6% interest
72 ÷ 9 = 8
Years to double at 9% interest
