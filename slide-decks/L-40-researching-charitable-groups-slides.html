Why Research Charities?

4 / 16

Not All Charities Are Equal

Charitable giving is powerful, but not all organizations use donations with the same efficiency or effectiveness.

Some may allocate large portions of funds to administrative costs rather than direct programs.

Others might not be legitimate at all—charity scams cost Americans hundreds of millions of dollars each year.

💡 By developing research skills, you become a more informed and impactful donor.

Charity Evaluation Tools

5 / 16

Independent Resources for Research

Several organizations provide free tools to help you research and compare charitable groups:

  • 🌟 Charity Navigator: Star ratings, financial health metrics, 9,000+ charities evaluated
  • 📋 GuideStar: Access to Form 990 tax returns, transparency ratings, 1.8M+ organizations
  • ✓ BBB Wise Giving Alliance: Evaluates against 20 accountability standards
  • 📊 CharityWatch: Grades on financial efficiency, highlights concerns like excessive reserves

💡 Use multiple sources—each tool evaluates charities using different criteria, giving you a more complete picture.

Evaluating Financial Health

6 / 16

Key Metrics to Review

Program Expense Ratio: What % of funds goes directly to programs vs. overhead? (70%+ is generally good)

Fundraising Efficiency: How much does it cost to raise $1? (Under $0.25 is efficient)

Working Capital: Does the organization have enough resources to sustain operations?

Executive Compensation: Are leadership salaries reasonable for the organization's size?

Understanding Form 990

The IRS Form 990 is a tax document nonprofits must file annually. It reveals:

  • 📊 Revenue sources
  • 💰 Expenses and program costs
  • 🏢 Assets and liabilities
  • 👔 Executive compensation
  • 📝 Mission and activities

Access Form 990s free on GuideStar or the IRS website.

The PACED Decision-Making Model

7 / 16

A Structured Approach for Comparing Charities

P
PROBLEM
Define the cause you want to support
A
ALTERNATIVES
Identify organizations addressing that cause
C
CRITERIA
Establish what matters most to you
E
EVALUATE
Compare each org against your criteria
D
DECIDE
Choose the org that best aligns

💡 Your criteria might include: program effectiveness, financial health, local vs. global impact, transparency, alignment with values.

Red Flags and Warning Signs

8 / 16

⚠️ Be Cautious of Organizations That:

  • Pressure you for immediate donations
  • Refuse to provide detailed information about their work
  • Have no clear mission or measurable outcomes
  • Lack transparency in financial reporting
  • Have names similar to well-known organizations
  • Cannot provide tax ID numbers
  • Have unusually high administrative costs
  • Offer limited leadership/governance information
#1
Scam Tactic: Pressure for Immediate Donations

✓ Legitimate Charities Will:

  • Give you time to research
  • Provide detailed program info
  • Share financial statements
  • Have verifiable tax-exempt status

Real-World Example

9 / 16

Maria's PACED Analysis

Maria has $500 to donate for environmental conservation. She researched two organizations using evaluation tools:

EcoAction: 82% to programs, local focus, 15 years operating, strong financial health, limited outcome data

Green Planet Alliance: 75% to programs, global policy focus, 8 years, detailed impact reports, rapid growth

Using PACED, Maria decided EcoAction better aligned with her values—she prefers supporting local efforts with lower overhead.

Her Decision Process

P: Support environmental conservation

A: EcoAction vs. Green Planet Alliance

C: Local impact, low admin costs, program focus

E: EcoAction scored higher on her criteria

D: Donated $500 + signed up to volunteer

💡 Neither charity was "wrong"—it depends on YOUR priorities and values.

Real-World Example

10 / 16

John Identifies a Scam

John received a call from "Children's Cancer Relief Fund" pressuring him for an immediate credit card donation with a "matching gift deadline."

Instead of donating immediately, John:

  • 1. Asked for website and tax ID
  • 2. Researched on Charity Navigator & GuideStar
  • 3. Found multiple red flags

Red Flags Discovered

  • ❌ Not registered as 501(c)(3)
  • ❌ No Form 990 tax returns available
  • ❌ Name similar to legitimate charity
  • ❌ No physical address listed
  • ❌ No leadership information
  • ❌ Hastily created website

✓ John reported it to authorities and donated to a legitimate childhood cancer charity instead.
