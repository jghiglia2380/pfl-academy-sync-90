Federal vs. Private Loans

9 / 20

Understanding Your Loan Options

If borrowing is necessary, federal loans should always be your first choice. They offer significant advantages over private loans.

  • Federal Subsidized: Government pays interest while you're in school (need-based)
  • Federal Unsubsidized: Available to all students; interest accrues immediately
  • Income-Driven Repayment: Federal loans offer flexible plans based on your income
  • Loan Forgiveness: Public Service Loan Forgiveness after 120 qualifying payments
  • Private Loans: Higher rates, less flexibility, often require cosigner
  • Credit Checks: Federal loans don't require credit history; private loans do

FAFSA: Your Gateway to Aid

10 / 20

Why FAFSA Is Essential

The Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) is the primary gateway to nearly all federal financial aid.

Completing the FAFSA determines eligibility for federal grants, loans, and work-study. Many states and colleges also use it to award their own aid.

Even if you think your family makes "too much," always complete the FAFSA—you may qualify for aid you didn't expect.

📅 Opens October 1st each year. Submit early—some aid is first-come, first-served!

Scholarship Landscape Today

12 / 20

Scholarships Aren't Just for "Perfect" Students

One of the biggest misconceptions is that scholarships are only for students with perfect grades or exceptional athletic ability.

In reality, scholarships exist for nearly every interest, background, and talent—from community service to unusual hobbies to specific career paths.

The key is starting early and applying strategically. Treat scholarship applications like a part-time job.
