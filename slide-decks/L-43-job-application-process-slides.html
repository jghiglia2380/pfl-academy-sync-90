Key Terms

4 / 18
Applicant Tracking System (ATS)
Definition

Software used by employers to collect, sort, scan, and filter job applications based on keywords and qualifications.

Why It Matters

Up to 75% of resumes are filtered out by ATS before a human ever sees them

Keywords
Definition

Specific terms in job descriptions that highlight required skills, qualifications, and experience that ATS scans for.

Examples

"Project management," "JavaScript," "Customer service," "Leadership," "Budget analysis"

Key Terms

5 / 18
Cover Letter
Definition

A document submitted alongside a resume that introduces the applicant and highlights relevant qualifications.

Purpose

Explains WHY you want this job and HOW your experience makes you the ideal candidate

Networking
Definition

Building professional relationships to exchange information, advice, support, and job referrals.

Statistic

~70% of people land jobs through networking connections rather than applications alone

Decoding Job Descriptions

9 / 18

Key Components to Analyze

  • Job Title – Official name of the position
  • Company Overview – Brief description of organization
  • Job Summary – Overview of the role's purpose
  • Responsibilities – Day-to-day tasks and expectations
  • Required Qualifications – Must-have education, experience, skills
  • Preferred Qualifications – Nice-to-have but not mandatory
  • Compensation & Benefits – Salary range (not always included)
  • Application Instructions – How to apply and what to submit

📌 Rule of Thumb: Apply if you meet ~70-80% of the requirements, especially the essential ones. Don't self-select out!

Customizing Your Application

10 / 18

Resume Customization

1. Prioritize Experience: Rearrange sections to highlight most relevant experience first

2. Integrate Keywords: Incorporate exact terms from the job description

3. Align Achievements: Emphasize accomplishments that demonstrate required skills

4. Tailor Summary: Customize your professional summary for each position

Cover Letter Essentials

Opening: Introduce yourself and specify the position

Why This Company: Show genuine interest and research

Your Fit: Highlight 2-3 key qualifications with specific examples

Call to Action: Express enthusiasm and request interview

⚠️ Never send the same generic application to multiple jobs!

Navigating Applicant Tracking Systems

11 / 18

📐 Standard Formatting

  • Simple, clean layouts
  • Standard section headings
  • Common fonts (Arial, Calibri)
  • Avoid tables and text boxes

🔑 Keyword Optimization

  • Include exact keywords from job description
  • Use both spelled-out and acronyms
  • Place keywords in context
  • Don't stuff or overuse terms

📄 File Format

  • Use .docx or .pdf (check instructions)
  • Professional file name
  • "JohnSmith_Resume.pdf"
  • Avoid special characters

✅ Follow Instructions

  • Complete ALL required fields
  • Answer all questions
  • Submit all documents
  • Double-check before submitting

Professional Follow-Up

12 / 18

Why Follow Up?

Professional follow-up demonstrates your continued interest and proactive attitude.

Timing: Wait 1-2 weeks after the application deadline

Method: Email is typically most appropriate

Tone: Polite, brief, and professional

Content: Reference specific position, express interest, offer additional info

Sample Follow-Up Email:

Subject: Follow-Up on [Position Title] - [Your Name]

Dear [Hiring Manager],

I recently applied for the [Position] role at [Company] and wanted to express my continued interest. My experience in [relevant skill] aligns well with your requirements, and I'm excited about the opportunity to [specific aspect].

Please let me know if you need any additional information.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
