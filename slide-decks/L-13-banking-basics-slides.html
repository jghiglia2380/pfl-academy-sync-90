Comparing Payment Methods

7 / 20

Checks

Best for: Large payments, rent, situations requiring documentation

Processing time: 1-5 business days to clear

Security: Can be stopped if lost; creates paper trail

Debit Cards

Best for: Everyday purchases, in-store and online shopping

Processing time: Instant deduction from account

Security: PIN protection; report lost cards immediately

ATMs

Best for: Cash withdrawals, quick balance checks, deposits

Processing time: Instant for withdrawals

Security: Use in-network ATMs; protect your PIN

Online Banking

Best for: Bill payments, transfers, account monitoring

Processing time: Same-day to 1-3 days for transfers

Security: Use secure networks; enable 2-factor authentication

Strategies to Avoid Fees

9 / 20

Smart Banking Means Fewer Fees

With a few simple habits, you can eliminate most banking fees and keep more of your hard-earned money:

Track Your Balance

Check your account regularly through online banking or the mobile app. Set up low balance alerts to warn you before you overspend.

Use In-Network ATMs

Find your bank's ATM locator on their app. Plan ahead to avoid using out-of-network machines that charge extra fees.

Meet Requirements

Maintain minimum balances or set up direct deposit. Many banks waive monthly fees with qualifying activities. Consider credit unions like {{STATE_LOCAL_CREDIT_UNION_NAME}} in {{STATE_NAME}} which often offer lower fees.
