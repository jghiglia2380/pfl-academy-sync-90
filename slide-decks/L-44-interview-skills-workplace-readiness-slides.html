Key Terms

4 / 20
Behavioral Interview
Definition

An interview approach focused on how candidates handled specific situations in the past to predict future performance.

Example Question

"Tell me about a time when you had to resolve a conflict with a coworker."

STAR Method
Definition

A structured technique for answering behavioral questions: Situation, Task, Action, and Result.

Why It Works

Provides clear, organized responses with concrete evidence of your capabilities.

Key Terms

5 / 20
Panel Interview
Definition

An interview conducted by multiple interviewers at once, often including team members, managers, and HR.

Pro Tip

Make eye contact with the person asking each question but include others in your responses.

Professional Etiquette
Definition

Expected behaviors in workplace settings that demonstrate respect, courtesy, and professionalism.

Examples

Punctuality, appropriate attire, active listening, respectful communication, thank-you notes.

The STAR Method Framework

7 / 20
S

Situation

Describe the context or background of the specific event or situation you're discussing

T

Task

Explain your responsibility or role in that situation—what were you expected to do?

A

Action

Detail the specific actions YOU took to address the task—focus on your contributions

R

Result

Share the outcomes of your actions—use specific metrics and numbers when possible

Common Interview Formats

Know What to Expect

Different interview formats require different preparation strategies. Understanding the format helps you prepare appropriately.

One-on-One: Focus on building rapport with a single interviewer. Most common format.

Panel: Multiple interviewers—note names and roles for follow-up.

Virtual: Test technology, ensure good lighting, look at camera.

Professional Appearance & Body Language

9 / 20

First Impressions Are Formed in Seconds

Research shows that interviewers form initial impressions within 7 seconds. Your appearance and body language communicate confidence, professionalism, and reliability before you say a word.

  • Greeting: Firm handshake, warm smile, confident posture
  • Posture: Sit straight but comfortable, slightly lean forward
  • Eye Contact: Maintain natural eye contact without staring
  • Hand Placement: Rest on table or lap, avoid crossed arms
  • Dress Code: One level above everyday attire for the position
  • Speaking Pace: Moderate pace with clear articulation

⚠️ Avoid: Fidgeting, checking phone/watch, slouching, excessive gestures, crossing arms defensively

Managing Interview Anxiety

10 / 20

Nervous? That's Normal!

Interview anxiety is completely natural—it shows you care about the opportunity. The key is managing it effectively so it doesn't interfere with your performance.

Preparation is your best weapon against anxiety. When you know your material, you can focus on connecting with the interviewer.

🧘 Deep breathing before interviews can reduce anxiety by up to 40% and improve cognitive performance.

93%
Candidates Feel Nervous

Anxiety Management Tips:

  • Arrive 10-15 minutes early
  • Practice deep breathing techniques
  • Use positive visualization
  • Prepare thoroughly in advance
  • Get good sleep the night before

Why Interview Skills Matter

11 / 20

Interviews Are Your Gateway to Career Success

Interview skills are career-long assets. Whether you're applying for your first job, seeking a promotion, or pivoting to a new career, your ability to present yourself confidently and competently will determine your opportunities.

  • Career Entry: Strong interviews open doors to better starting positions
  • Salary Negotiation: Confident candidates earn 7-10% more in starting salary
  • Promotions: Internal candidates interview too—same skills apply
  • Career Pivots: Transferable skills require clear articulation
  • Networking: Every professional conversation is a mini-interview
  • Client Relationships: First impressions extend to business development

💰 The Financial Impact: A strong first job can set your salary trajectory for years. Starting $5,000 higher can mean $500,000+ over a career.

Questions to Ask the Interviewer

Jamie's STAR Method Success

Jamie interviewed for a marketing assistant position. When asked "Tell me about a time you worked under a tight deadline," she used the STAR method to structure her response:

Before STAR: "I'm good under pressure and have worked on lots of tight deadlines."

With STAR: "During my internship, our team was asked to create a social media campaign in 5 days instead of 3 weeks. I created a day-by-day schedule, completed all 10 posts ahead of schedule, and the client approved without revisions."
