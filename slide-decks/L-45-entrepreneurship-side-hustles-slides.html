Key Terms

Entrepreneurship
Definition

The activity of setting up and running a business, taking on financial risks in the hope of profit.

Example

Starting a web design agency, opening a bakery, launching an app company

Side Hustle
Definition

A way to make money alongside your primary job, often leveraging personal skills or interests.

Example

Freelance writing on weekends, tutoring after work, selling crafts online

Key Terms

5 / 20
MVP
Minimum Viable Product

A version of a product with just enough features to be usable by early customers and provide feedback.

Why It Matters

Test your idea quickly and cheaply before investing significant time or money.

Bootstrapping
Definition

Building a company from the ground up using personal finances and revenue—no outside investors.

Advantage

You maintain full ownership and control of your business decisions and profits.

Identifying Opportunities

9 / 20

Where Do Business Ideas Come From?

Successful entrepreneurs find opportunities by looking at their own experiences, skills, and observations of problems around them.

  • Personal Experience: Problems you've encountered needing better solutions
  • Skills & Expertise: Areas where you have specialized knowledge
  • Hobbies & Interests: Activities you enjoy and understand well
  • Market Trends: Emerging needs or changes in consumer behavior
  • Technology Changes: New capabilities enabling different products
  • Underserved Markets: Groups whose needs aren't fully met

💡 Key Question: What problem do I see every day that I'm uniquely positioned to solve?

Evaluating Your Ideas

10 / 20

Not All Ideas Make Good Businesses

Before investing time and money, evaluate your idea using these criteria:

Problem Validation: Is there a real problem? How painful is it?

Market Size: How many people have this problem?

Feasibility: Do you have the skills and resources needed?

Profitability: Can you deliver at a price that makes profit?

  • Scalability: Can it grow beyond you?

Why Side Hustles Matter Financially

11 / 20

Diversifying Your Income Creates Security

Relying on a single income source is risky. Side hustles provide financial resilience and accelerate wealth building.

  • Extra Income: Average side hustler earns $810/month additional
  • Debt Payoff: Use extra income to eliminate debt faster
  • Emergency Fund: Build financial cushion more quickly
  • Investing: Additional capital for retirement savings
  • Skills Development: Marketable experience for career growth
  • Business Asset: Potential to sell or scale into full-time

💰 $810/month invested at 8% for 30 years = $1.1 million—side hustles can fund your retirement.

From Idea to Implementation

12 / 20

Take Action: Start Small and Learn

1. Market Research: Interview potential customers about their needs before building anything.

2. Create MVP: Build the simplest version that delivers value and get feedback.

3. Basic Business Plan: Outline your value proposition, target market, and revenue model.

4. Start Small: Serve a few customers, document what works, then gradually expand.

Hours/Week to Start

MVP Mindset:

  • Focus only on core functionality
  • Eliminate nice-to-have features
  • Test assumptions quickly
  • Iterate based on feedback

Real-World Example

14 / 20
👩‍🎨
Maya's Jewelry Business Journey

Maya worked full-time as an admin assistant while making jewelry as a hobby. Friends encouraged her to sell, so she started on Etsy with 10 designs.

Strategic growth over 3 years:

  • Year 1: $500-800/month (Etsy only)
  • Year 2: $1,200-1,800/month (+ craft fairs)
  • Year 3: $3,000-4,000/month (own website + boutiques)

After saving 6 months of expenses, she went full-time.
