Key Terms

4 / 20

Identity Theft

The fraudulent acquisition and use of a person's private identifying information for financial gain

Example: Someone opens credit cards in your name using your stolen Social Security number

Data Breach

An incident where information is stolen from a system without authorization

Example: Hackers steal customer information from a retailer's database, exposing millions

Key Terms

5 / 20

Credit Freeze

A security measure that restricts access to your credit report, blocking new account openings

Example: With a freeze, even someone with your SSN can't open credit in your name

Skimming

Illegal copying of information from credit or debit card magnetic strips using hidden devices

Example: A device placed on an ATM captures your card data when you insert it

Digital vs. Physical Theft Methods

7 / 20

Digital Methods

  • Phishing emails and fake websites
  • Data breaches at companies
  • Malware and keyloggers
  • Social media information mining
  • Public Wi-Fi interception
  • Account credential stuffing

Physical Methods

  • Dumpster diving through trash
  • Mail theft from mailboxes
  • ATM/gas pump skimming devices
  • Stolen wallet or purse
  • Shoulder surfing at ATMs
  • Social engineering in person

Prevention: Secure Your Information

9 / 20

Proactive Protection

  • Strong, unique passwords for each account

  • Be selective about sharing info on social media
  • Use a locked mailbox or P.O. box
  • Shred sensitive documents before disposal
  • Avoid public Wi-Fi for financial transactions

Case Study: Maria's Credit Card Fraud

13 / 20

The Discovery

Maria checked her credit card statement online and found nearly $3,000 in purchases she didn't make - electronics ordered from across the country and shipped to an unfamiliar address.

Maria's Immediate Actions

  • Called credit card company to report fraud
  • Reviewed all other financial accounts
  • Changed passwords on financial accounts
  • Placed fraud alert on credit reports

The Resolution

  • Fraudulent charges removed after investigation
  • New card issued with different number
  • Source identified: data breach at online retailer
  • Now uses dedicated card for online shopping

Case Study: David's Tax Identity Theft

14 / 20

The Discovery

David filed his tax return in April only to receive an IRS notice that a return had already been filed using his Social Security number. Someone had claimed his refund.

The Challenges

  • IRS Form 14039 required (Identity Theft Affidavit)
  • Had to file legitimate return by paper mail
  • Resolution took approximately 8 months
  • Multiple verification requests to respond to

David's Recovery Steps

  • Placed credit freeze with all 3 bureaus
  • Filed reports with FTC and local police
  • Contacted Social Security Administration
  • Now has IRS Identity Protection PIN
